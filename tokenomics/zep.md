# ZEP tokenomics

> The ZEP supply cap, who receives it, and the 21-year release schedule.

ZEP has a hard cap of 1,000,000,000. 21,000,000 are unlocked at genesis for the ICO, 50,000,000 vest to investors over four years, and the remaining 929,000,000 are released over 21 years, with each year releasing 10% less than the one before.

:::note On the devnet
The devnet pays the validator column on-chain, 299 of every 979 ZEP minted, and sends everything else to one foundation wallet. The split between liquidity, foundation, team and investors, the cliffs and the investor vesting are not enforced on-chain yet; see [Validators and rewards](/validators/overview).
:::

## The schedule in short

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Hard cap | 1,000,000,000 ZEP |
| Unlocked at genesis | 21,000,000 ZEP (ICO) |
| Released on the 21-year curve | 929,000,000 ZEP |
| Yearly decrease | 10% |
| Curve years | 21 |
| Year 1 curve release | 104,313,924 ZEP |
| Investor vesting | 4 years |
| Cliff, Team and Investors | 12 months |

## Who receives it

| Share | Of the 1B cap | ZEP | Release | Cliff |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Validators | 29.9% | 299,000,000 | 21-year curve | None |
| Liquidity | 21% | 210,000,000 | 21-year curve | None |
| Foundation | 21% | 210,000,000 | 21-year curve | None |
| Team & Contributors | 21% | 210,000,000 | 21-year curve | 1 year |
| Investors | 5% | 50,000,000 | Even over 4 years | 1 year |
| ICO | 2.1% | 21,000,000 | At genesis | None |

Team and Investors accrue from genesis, but nothing is liquid during the first 12 months. What they accrued in year 1 unlocks when the cliff ends, so it is counted in year 2.
